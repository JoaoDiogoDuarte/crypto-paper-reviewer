Keyboard shortcuts

Click Change, then press the new combination (Esc cancels). Shortcuts are saved for you, for every paper. Firefox keeps Ctrl+W, Ctrl+Q, Ctrl+T, Ctrl+N and Ctrl+Shift+R for itself.

Editing

  • Type anywhere in the text. Enter starts a new paragraph; Backspace at the start of a paragraph joins it to the one before.
  • Ctrl+M: inline maths (of the selection, if any; $ over a selection too, otherwise $ types a dollar sign). Ctrl+Shift+M: displayed maths.
  • A bracket, brace or quote typed over a selection wraps it. Pasted $…$ becomes maths.
  • Ctrl+Alt+C: cite (search by key, title, author or year; Ctrl+Enter picks several; not found? search online and add it to references.bib).
  • Ctrl+Alt+R: reference a section, theorem, figure, table or equation.
  • Ctrl+F: find and replace. Ctrl+\: insert any LaTeX.
  • Ctrl+I / Ctrl+B: \emph / \textbf.
  • Click maths, a reference or any grey chip to edit its LaTeX; Ctrl+Enter applies.
  • Ctrl+click a reference or citation: go to it (Lemma 4, the equation, the reference entry); Alt+← goes back.
  • Hover a block and use ＋ to add a paragraph, heading or displayed maths after it, or ✕ to delete a heading.
  • Heading tools move sections up or down and combine a section with the one before it; put the cursor in a section and use Split section to divide it.
  • Headings, figure and table captions are edited in place; listings in their box. quote/quotation blocks show indented, and are edited in place too.
  • Games: Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Shift+Z undo or redo a draft; Alt+Up/Down moves a safe statement. Ctrl+Enter applies the figure; Ctrl+S applies and saves.
  • Game panels can be dragged or moved with Left/Right only when their LaTeX blocks are safe siblings. Mark uses xcolor boxes.
  • Ctrl+S saves. Saving writes only the files you changed, applies the house rules, and rebuilds the PDF.

Reading aloud

  • Click in a sentence and press ▶ (or Alt+R); use « and » to move between sentences. Maths is read as words.
