Editing

  • Type anywhere in the text. Enter starts a new paragraph; Backspace at the start of a paragraph joins it to the one before.
  • $ or Ctrl+M: inline maths. Ctrl+Shift+M: displayed maths.
  • Ctrl+Alt+C: cite (search by key, title, author or year; Ctrl+Enter picks several; not found? search online and add it to references.bib).
  • Ctrl+Alt+R: reference a section, theorem, figure, table or equation.
  • Ctrl+F: find and replace. Ctrl+\: insert any LaTeX.
  • Ctrl+I / Ctrl+B: \emph / \textbf.
  • Click maths, a reference or any grey chip to edit its LaTeX; Ctrl+Enter applies.
  • Hover a block and use ＋ to add a paragraph, heading or displayed maths after it, or ✕ to delete a heading.
  • Headings, figure and table captions are edited in place; listings in their box.
  • Ctrl+S saves. Saving writes only the files you changed, applies the house rules, and rebuilds the PDF.

Reading aloud

  • Put the cursor in a paragraph and press ▶ (or Alt+R). Maths is read as words.
